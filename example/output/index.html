An Outside-In View

An honest assessment of what the numbers say, what they don't, and where the portfolio stands heading into H2 2026.

The Central Question
Summit's DSCR is 0.92x. The business generates $670K of SDE against $732K of annual debt service. That is a $62K/yr gap. SDE margin has improved from 12% to 16% in six quarters, which is real operational progress. But revenue has declined from $1.18M/quarter to $1.0M over the same period. Can Summit close the $62K gap before the cash reserve runs out?

The Evidence

1. Greenfield carries the portfolio, and time is compounding.
~$3.3M net equity in Greenfield at 7.5x midpoint. The other two funds combined: roughly breakeven. Revenue up 12% organically. SDE margin compressed from 15.5% to 11% mid-2024 (add-on labor scaling), now recovering to 12%. Debt pays off Jan 2030. At current SDE of $822K, FCFADS goes from $181K today to $822K when the debt clears. That is a 4.5x increase in free cash flow with no operational improvement.
2. Summit: the margins are improving, the revenue isn't.
SDE margin went from 12% to 18.2% in six quarters, which is genuine operational improvement. But revenue declined from $1.18M/quarter at acquisition to ~$1.0M. At current margins, the business needs ~$4.5M annual revenue to clear 1.0x DSCR. TTM is $4.12M. The gap is $62K/yr of debt service coverage. The business operates fine. The balance sheet is heavy for the current revenue level.
3. Riverstone is the clean one. Three quarters is too early to tell.
DSCR at 2.92x, margins at 16%, no drama. But three quarters of data is not a trend. The question is whether the $890K equity deployment earns its keep over 3-5 years.
4. Capital deployment is progressing, but recycling has not started.
$2.1M deployed across 3 acquisitions. $450K of salary distributions across two companies over 6 quarters. $31K reinvested into Summit operations. No profit distributions or capital recycled between companies. The platform is building (3 companies in 3 years), but reinvestment from operating cash has not yet begun at scale.

Recommendations

1. Push Summit revenue past $4.5M.
Highest urgency. $62K/yr short of DSCR breakeven. At current 16% SDE margin, every $100K of revenue adds $16K of SDE. Need ~$390K more annual revenue. Levers: lead gen, close rates, average ticket size. Consider a seasonal marketing push in Q3 when cleaning demand peaks.
2. Get Greenfield SDE margin back to 13-14%.
Highest value creation. 2 points of margin recovery on $7.2M = $145K additional SDE, worth $1.1M of enterprise value at 7.5x. The key lever is labor utilization: tightening scheduling, reducing rework. Pricing and route density have presumably been reviewed post add-ons.
3. Track FCFADS monthly. Set a reinvestment threshold.
Define a portfolio-wide threshold: once monthly FCFADS passes it, split excess between profit distributions and a reinvestment pool. Even $5K/month set aside counts. The first dollar of reinvested surplus matters more than the amount.

Grow & Harvest

Three paths forward. The right answer depends on whether Summit stabilizes and how quickly Greenfield's margins recover.

Path A: Organic Focus

No new acquisitions. Fix Summit's revenue, recover Greenfield's margins, let Riverstone compound. Redirect all surplus FCFADS to debt paydown and reinvestment.

  • Lowest risk, lowest capital need
  • Summit hits DSCR breakeven by Q4 2026
  • Greenfield margin recovery adds $1.1M EV
  • No new equity calls
